# AxisOption Community Standards

AxisOption expects professional, respectful, and security-conscious communication across its public GitHub presence.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Communicate respectfully and stay focused on the technical or documentation topic
- Provide constructive, specific feedback
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and applicable law
- Avoid publishing sensitive account, payment, security, or personal information
- Use official support and security channels when a matter should not be public
- Clearly distinguish personal opinions from official AxisOption statements

## Unacceptable behavior

The following may be removed or restricted:

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or personal attacks
- Spam, deceptive promotion, impersonation, or phishing
- Publication of credentials, private keys, recovery phrases, personal data, or confidential material
- Security exploitation instructions directed at real users or systems without authorization
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or support-routing processes
- False claims presented as official AxisOption announcements

## Security-sensitive matters

Potential vulnerabilities must not be disclosed publicly before review. Follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Client and business matters

GitHub discussions are not a substitute for account support or confidential business communication.

- Client support: https://axisoption.com/contact.html
- Partner enquiries: https://axisoption.com/partners.html

## Moderation

AxisOption may edit, hide, close, or remove content that violates these standards and may restrict participation where necessary to protect users, contributors, or the integrity of its public channels.

These standards apply to public interaction within AxisOption-managed GitHub repositories unless a repository publishes more specific rules.
