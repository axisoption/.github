## Newsroom Principles

AxisOption's GitHub newsroom follows five rules:

1. **Official sources first** — links point to AxisOption-owned pages whenever possible.
2. **Archive dates stay explicit** — historical milestones are not presented as newly launched features.
3. **Current pages govern current availability** — product and legal pages take precedence over historical archive coverage.
4. **No guaranteed-return messaging** — trading is presented with appropriate risk context.
5. **No private operational disclosure** — proprietary infrastructure, credentials, user data and security-sensitive information are not published here.
