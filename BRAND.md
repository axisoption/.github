# AxisOption Brand & Public References

This file defines the preferred public references for the AxisOption name, links, and visual identity across GitHub and other public technical materials.

## Official name

Use **AxisOption** as the primary brand name.

When referring to the public platform, prefer clear product language such as:

- AxisOption trading platform
- AxisOption interactive demo
- AxisOption Partner Program
- AxisOption News Hub

Avoid presenting third-party services, unofficial profiles, mirrors, or community accounts as official AxisOption properties.

## Brand integrity

When creating public material that references AxisOption:

- Do not alter product claims into guarantees or promises of financial return
- Do not describe an unofficial website, application, social account, or service as an AxisOption property
- Do not remove risk context where it is relevant to trading-related promotional material
- Do not fabricate regulatory, licensing, performance, security, or user-statistic claims
- Prefer links to the official website for current product, legal, partner, and support information
